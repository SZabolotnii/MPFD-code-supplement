Mixed-Parity Fractional Dictionaries for Moment-Based Discrimination:
Parity, Admissibility, and Exponent Selection
Serhii Zabolotnii

- Parity, not the split of the support, decides a moment discriminant's dictionary
- An all-odd dictionary is exactly blind to two classes symmetric about the origin
- An all-even dictionary is exactly blind to a mirror-skew pair of classes
- Two difference curves show which parity carries the difference before any fitting
- Contamination caps every exponent at half the contaminant's tail index
